# Sakura-Spring-Japanese-Cherry-Blossom-Anime-Animation
A beautiful Japanese spring anime animation featuring cherry blossoms in full bloom, falling pink petals, peaceful rivers, traditional Japanese architecture, and a walking anime character. Experience the beauty of Sakura season through a cinematic animated world inspired by Japan.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser — no build step, no dependencies.

- Cherry trees: layered blossom clusters with individual flowers sway in the wind, plus a detailed blossom branch in the foreground
- Petals: notched sakura petals fall and flutter through the air and float down the river
- River: live, wobbling reflections of the sky, trees and lights, with ripples, glints and rings around the rocks
- Anime girl: in a furisode kimono with a paper parasol, walks the stone path, crosses the bridge and goes home
- Thatched house with glowing shoji screens, arched wooden bridge, lanterns, mossy rocks, a pagoda and forested hills at dusk
- Sound: water, wind and birdsong, synthesised live with the Web Audio API (press "Start with sound")
